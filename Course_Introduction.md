# Course Introduction

## About the Course
CPELX230-330 introduces Agentic Software Engineering: building software with AI agents that perceive, reason, and act toward goals. The course covers software engineering foundations (Sommerville), Agile and Scrum, version control with Git, intelligent agents (Russell and Norvig), and responsible AI using the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.

## About Me
I am Nathan Escano, a Computer Engineering student (COE231/COE07) at National University. I am interested in becoming a web developer. I am taking this course to learn how modern software teams build AI-powered systems safely and collaboratively, since AI agents are becoming part of how web applications are built and run.

## What I Expect

- Practice Agile teamwork and Git-based collaboration
